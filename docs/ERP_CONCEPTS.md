# ERP concepts in this simulation

- **Requisition:** internal request that describes an intended purchase and its business purpose.
- **Purchase order:** approved commitment sent to a supplier, with quantities, agreed prices, and expected delivery.
- **Goods receipt note (GRN):** record of deliveries against a PO. This simulation captures delivered, accepted, and rejected quantities.
- **Accounts payable (AP):** the invoice intake and liability validation stage before payment.
- **Two-way match:** compare invoice quantities and prices to the purchase order.
- **Three-way match:** compare the invoice to both the purchase order and accepted receipt evidence.
- **Invoice exception:** a difference or missing source record that needs review before approval.
- **Approval workflow:** recorded decision by an assigned demo role before a requisition, PO, or matched invoice advances.
- **Reconciliation:** compare invoice totals with payment rows to derive paid and remaining balances.
- **Audit trail:** timestamped business events showing workflow actions and the stated actor.

Names and processes here are educational and do not assert product parity with Oracle Fusion.
